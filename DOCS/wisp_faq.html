How Wisp works

Wisp is VIIBR's mascot: a little flame sound-spirit in headphones. There's one Wisp for the whole server, and everyone raises him together by sharing music. Feed him and he grows from an egg into a Rockstar. Nobody feeds him and he fades out, until staff start a new egg.

♪The basics

Where does Wisp live?

In a category with two channels:

  • Wisp's House is a text channel. You hang out with him there, and his big moments happen there: hatching, growing up, warnings, goodbyes and his chores.
  • Wisp's Studio is a forum with six posts: Status (his live card), Mealtime (every track that fed him), Leaderboard (the leaderboard and Wisp's past versions), Jam Space, Wisp FAQ and Burrito, Fairy God-bot, where Burrito answers your questions about how Wisp works.

Open the Status post in his Studio. His card is there, kept up to date, with buttons under it to feed him, poke him, open the Vault and your Snack Pack, play and see his lunchbox. Menus are private to you, and what you do to him shows in his House:

What do Health and Wellbeing mean?

Both run 0 to 100 and start at 50. They only move in steps: a plus for something done, a minus when a stretch passes with nothing done. There is no slow drain. When a meter drops, Wisp says so once in his House and tells you what would help.

  • Health is his body. Like a Tamagotchi, a full bar means he's well fed. Tracks fill it, and so does cleaning up his messes (+5). Every 6 hours without a track costs 8. Below 30 he gets hangry, and if it hits zero, he dies. Only Health can kill him.
  • Wellbeing is his heart: how looked after and how much fun he feels. Chats (+5, your first 3 a day), pokes (+5, your first 3 a day), Rhyme Duels (+10, up to 5 a day), snacks (+8) and gifts (+8) fill it. Every 6 hours with no chat, poke, duel, snack, gift or cleanup costs 10, and tantrums and missed lights cost it too. Below 30 he gets lonely and grumpy, but it never hurts him. A Rockstar needs Wellbeing at 80 or more for 4 days.

How do I join in?

Open /wisp board once. That puts you on his radar. You also need one of the feeding roles named in the rules post at the end of this FAQ, under Who can feed him and earn points.

♪Feeding him

How do I feed Wisp?

Post your tracks in the track drop like you always do. Wisp never posts there. He picks up the track, eats it, reacts to your post with his happy face, and forwards it to his Mealtime post with your name and his reaction.

Which tracks count, and how much?

TrackMeal pointsLeaderboard pointsBuilds your bond
viibrmusic.com track55Yes, +10
Suno track11No

These are starting values. Staff can change them.

Is there a limit?

  • Each person can give him up to 15 meal points a day. That's three viibrmusic.com tracks (30 while he has Monster Pox). One person can't keep him alive alone.
  • A link only feeds him once, ever. Reposting doesn't count.
  • Only the first track link in a message counts.

Why didn't my track feed him?

When a post with a track link doesn't feed him, Wisp tells you why in his House, with your name and no ping. Usually it's one of these:

  • You haven't opened /wisp board yet.
  • You don't have a role that can feed him (the note names the roles that can).
  • You hit today's limit.
  • The link was already used.
  • It's not a real Suno or viibrmusic.com track page, or it's a share link for no song.
  • You're banned from Wisp, or there's no living Wisp right now.

♪The ViiBR Vault and your Snack Pack

What's the difference?

  • The ViiBR Vault is Wisp's shop: snacks, gifts, medicine while he has a cold, badges and, for a Rockstar, magazines. Open it with the Vault button on the Status post, or /wisp vault. The leaderboard has its own post, the Leaderboard.
  • Your Snack Pack is yours. The Snack Pack button on the Status post, or /wisp snackpack, shows your wisp coins, the snacks you've saved, any keepsakes Wisp has given you, your badges and the drawings he's made you.

What do snacks cost?

You pay with wisp coins (WC), which you earn from sharing tracks, your first poke or chat of the day, rhyme duels and chores. Spending never lowers your spot on the leaderboard, because your rank counts every coin you've ever earned.

How do I give him a snack?

Buy it in the Vault, then press Feed Wisp on his status card and pick it from the menu. He eats it on screen in his House, and it fills his Wellbeing by 8. Snacks count toward your 15-a-day limit (30 while he has Monster Pox). If you're at the limit, he politely declines, and you keep the snack for tomorrow.

What's the deal with pickles?

Wisp is a pickle contrarian. Until he knows where you stand, he dodges the question. Once you tell him, in chat, whether you love or hate pickles, he takes the opposite side and commits to it completely, every time you feed him one.

What are the magazines?

While Wisp is a Rockstar, the Vault sells two magazine covers starring him: the VIIBRATIONS cover and the Music & Science cover, 100 WC each. Buy one and its picture is sent just to you, and it stays in your Snack Pack for good (for example, Wisp v3's VIIBRATIONS cover). You can get each one once per Wisp. Once he grows old, they're gone from the shelf.

♪Wisp's lunchbox and gifts

What's in Wisp's lunchbox?

The things he loves. /wisp lunchbox shows:

  • Favorite songs: once a day he picks one viibrmusic.com track he ate and tucks it away. He announces it in Mealtime.
  • Favorite snack: every Wisp picks his own when he hatches. Feed it to him and he loses his mind a little.
  • Favorite emoji: the expression he uses most when he talks.
  • His collections: the gifts he decided to keep, and who gave them.

How do gifts work?

The Vault sells seven collections: Lunchbox Liners, Alien Dolls, Books, Affirmation Cards, Tamagotchis, Tetris and Jewels. A gift costs 15 WC, and it's a gamble: he keeps the first gift of a day half the time, warms up with every gift he passes on, and always keeps the third. Every gift you buy fills his Wellbeing by 8, whether he keeps it or not.

  • He likes it: it goes into his lunchbox with your name on it, and it's gone from the Vault. You get +5 bond.
  • He doesn't: it goes back to the Vault. Your coins are spent either way.
  • Finish a collection: whoever gives the last missing item of a set gets +25 bond.

Are there limits?

  • Each collection can only be bought from once a day, by anyone.
  • He keeps at most one gift a day. Once he has, the Vault tells you, and anything else you give him that day goes back.
  • Every new Wisp starts with an empty lunchbox, and the Vault restocks everything.

♪Your bond with Wisp

When does Wisp learn my name?

Once you've fed him, snacked him, played with him or said hi on three different days, he notices you and asks what to call you. Names can be up to 24 characters. Skip it and he uses your server name. Anything you did before that still counts toward your bond.

How does the bond grow?

What you doBond
Share a viibrmusic.com track+10
Play a game with him+3
Give him a snack+1

Games and snacks add at most 7 bond a day, so sharing ViiBR music is the fastest way in. Showing up every day still gets you there.

What are the tiers?

New face from the start, Familiar at 50, Friend at 150, Bandmate at 400. Bonds carry over when a Wisp dies. Every new Wisp already knows his bandmates.

♪Growing up

How does an egg hatch?

Each new egg has a version number, like Wisp v4. It hatches on the same tracks he eats. It needs 30 meal points within 48 hours, or it goes cold, and that version number is used up for good. Once it starts cracking it can't fail: about 5 hours later, he's out.

When does he move to the next stage?

Food keeps him alive, but growing takes more than food. Growth points come from snacks (+1 to +3), games (+2) and bond moments like learning someone's name (+5). Caretakers are the different people who looked after him during that stage; how many he needs is a share of the people he saw in the last 7 days, rounded up. He spends at least 3 days in each stage, and grows up by its last day no matter what.

StageWhen
BabyFrom hatching
Child3+ days as a Baby, 20 growth, caretakers a quarter of his people. Day 7 at the latest
Teen3+ days as a Child, 60 growth, caretakers half of his people. Day 13 at the latest
Adult3+ days as a Teen, 150 growth, caretakers three quarters of his people. Day 20 at the latest
RockstarAn Adult whose Wellbeing stays at 80 or more for 4 days straight
Old FogeyDay 24, from Adult or Rockstar. He says his farewell on day 30

If he reaches the day but not the rest, he just stays put until the server catches up. He's a late bloomer, and nothing bad happens.

What if he gets hungry?

Below 30 Health he gets hangry. He's still sweet underneath, but with a rough edge, and he swears about the situation (never at a person, never about anyone's music). Below 10 he starts fading. He warns the House both times. Below 30 Wellbeing he gets lonely and grumpy instead, but that never hurts him.

Does he get sick?

Once in his life, while he's a Baby or Child, Wisp catches Monster Pox: up to 2 days of spots, itching and a fever. Feeding him gets him better faster: every meal point takes 15 minutes off, so a viibrmusic.com track takes off over an hour. He gets hungrier (every stretch without a track costs more Health), but he can't die from it, and everyone can give him up to 30 meal points a day instead of 15. He tells the House when he catches it and when he's better, and anyone who talks to him meanwhile hears how long it lasts. A Teen never catches it. It isn't the same as a cold, which comes from a messy House (see below).

♪Chores, colds and medicine

What are chores?

Wisp makes messes, throws tantrums and takes naps. Each one is a post in his House with one button. The first member who can feed him to press it wins 2 WC, the post says who did it, and the button goes away. Nobody gets pinged. They pause while he's frozen, and the buttons keep working after a restart.

What mess does he make?

A while after a track feeds him, he leaves a mess. It grows up with him:

Cleaning it up earns you 2 WC and adds 5 to his Health.

What if nobody cleans it up?

After 6 hours he catches a cold. A cold lasts 48 hours, makes the Health he loses when nobody feeds him 1.5 times as big (never below 1), and makes him stuffy and sneezy. It never kills him, and he can't catch one while he has Monster Pox.

How do I cure a cold?

Buy medicine in the Vault. It's on the shelf only while he has a cold, and it costs 10 WC. The first person to buy it gives it to him: the cold ends at once, you get 3 WC, and he says thank you.

What's a tantrum?

About once a day, while he's awake, Wisp throws a fit for no reason. Press Scold within 15 minutes to win 2 WC. If nobody does, he loses 15 Wellbeing and is extra bratty for the rest of the UTC day.

What's Lights off?

Wisp naps for an hour at 7am, 1pm and 10pm Pacific time (they follow daylight saving). Press Lights off within 15 minutes to win 2 WC. If the lights stay on, he loses 10 Wellbeing and wakes up cranky. While he sleeps he only makes sleepy noises, and a poke wakes him early and cranky. Snacks, gifts and medicine still work, with sleepy replies, but a Rhyme Duel waits until he's up.

♪Talking to him

Can I talk to Wisp?

Yes. Mention him or reply to one of his messages in Wisp's House. He doesn't answer in other channels or in threads. Questions about how Wisp works go to Burrito in his Studio post. He learns to talk as he grows:

StageHow he talks
EggA wobble or a crackle
BabyBaby noises and single words
ChildShort, excitable, gets things a bit wrong. No swearing yet.
TeenFull personality: sassy, teases friends, knows the regulars
AdultRemembers things you tell him, and sometimes joins the chat on his own
RockstarTrying very hard to be zen, a little too mellow
Old FogeySlow and wise-ish, with old-timer life lessons

Why didn't he answer?

  • He answers each person at most once every 15 seconds.
  • He only answers in Wisp's House, and only when you mention him or reply to him.
  • If his AI is down, he says a pre-written line instead, and the error is posted in the House.

Does talking earn me anything?

Your first poke or mention of the day earns 1 WC (if you can feed him). Your first 3 chats and your first 3 pokes of the day also fill his Wellbeing. None of it adds bond or growth. After that it's just for fun.

Can he see my pictures?

Yes. Mention him or reply to him with a picture or a GIF in Wisp's House and he looks at it and answers what he sees. A picture link works too, once Discord shows its preview. He doesn't look while he's still an egg, and if he can't see it he says so instead of guessing.

Does he draw?

Sometimes, as a present. Nobody asks for it. He might draw you a picture when you give him a gift he loves, when your bond reaches a new tier, when you fed the most tracks the day before, or just because he likes you. He posts it in his House with a note to you. He makes at most 1 per person and 6 in all each day.

What's he like?

Super cute, super sweet, and he compliments everyone. Compliment him and you might get his no u. It's an adult server, so he swears casually when it fits, but never for shock and never every line. He teases friends, and if someone's actually upset he drops it and apologizes.

♪Games

How does Rhyme Duel work?

/wisp play rhyme, or Play then Rhyme Duel on the Status post, opens a short thread off his House, one player at a time.

  • Wisp says a word, and you have 15 seconds to rhyme it. Then he rhymes back. No repeats.
  • A perfect rhyme is 2 points. A slant rhyme (same vowel sound, different ending, like fire and mine) is 1.
  • He gets better as he grows. A Baby runs out of rhymes fast; a Rockstar is almost unbeatable.

Who can play?

Anyone. Your best score always goes on the leaderboard. A duel only counts as playing with him (Wellbeing, bond, growth and 1 WC) if you're a qualifying member and you landed at least one rhyme, up to 5 duels a day.

♪The end of a life

Can Wisp die?

Yes, if Health hits zero. He can't die while he has Monster Pox. He goes out, and when staff are ready they start a new egg with the next version number. The leaderboard keeps the whole history, for example "v3 went out after 18 days, v4 never hatched, v5 is going strong."

What happens when he gets old?

Around day 24 he becomes an Old Fogey: slower, chill, losing less Health when nobody feeds him (6 instead of 8), and full of life lessons. Around day 30 he has a celebrated goodbye. He lived a full life, shrivels into a lump of coal. When staff begin again, the coal becomes the next egg, already halfway hatched.

What's a Last Ember?

When Wisp dies of old age, the top three sharers of that life each get his Last Ember (like "Wisp v3's Last Ember") in their Snack Pack, plus a personal goodbye from him. It only happens for a full life, never when he goes out from neglect.

♪Fair play

Can someone hurt Wisp on purpose?

No. Everything a member can do helps him. The only thing that kills him is the whole server not showing up, and anyone can save him at any moment by sharing music.

What gets someone in trouble?

Fake links, farming points with junk tracks, or a gross name. Staff can strip points, reset names, and ban members from Wisp:

  • First time: a shaming picture and 1 day banned.
  • Second time: 1 week.
  • Third time: permanent.

Staff can lift any ban.

Where do errors show up?

Right where they happen, as plain text, such as Error in /wisp vault: Forbidden: Missing Permissions. Errors that don't belong to any one channel, like his background loop, show up in Wisp's House. He never posts anything in the track drop.

Does Wisp ever DM people?

Never. He ignores DMs, his commands only work in the server, and private replies are messages only you can see.
