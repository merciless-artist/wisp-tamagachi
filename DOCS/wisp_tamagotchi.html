Wisp Tamagotchi

One Wisp for the whole server, raised by everyone sharing music. Fed well, he grows from an egg into a Rockstar. Neglected, he dies, and the next egg gets a new version number. Every life is a run the server tries to beat.

Numbers tagged starting value get tuned after a week of real data.

♪Where Wisp lives

A server category, Wisp's World, with one text channel and one forum.

  • Wisp's House is the hangout: talk to him, play, give snacks. Death, new eggs, cracking, hatching and stage-ups happen here.
  • Mealtime: members keep posting in the track drop channel as normal. Each qualifying Suno or viibrmusic.com track is forwarded here automatically and feeds him.
  • The Jam Space is where he talks music: his favorite songs from the community, every friendship song (one post each, maybe also shared to announcements), the lyrics he starts writing once he's older, and his song listens, the rarest and most expensive thing a member can get.
  • The Lunchbox also sells badges: role icons like “Team Wisp” that show by your name and raise your bond with him.

Food keeps him alive. Care makes him grow. Growth points come from everything that isn't a plain meal: snacks (+1, more for fancy ones), playtime (+2), and bond moments like learning someone's name or a friendship song (+5). Caretakers are the different people who interacted with him during that stage, so a couple of regulars can't raise him alone. If he reaches the day but not the rest, he stays put until the server catches up. He's a late bloomer, nothing bad happens. All numbers are starting values.

♪How Wisp eats

  • Where: members keep posting tracks in the tracks channel exactly as they do now. Wisp never posts or reacts there. Each track that feeds him is piped into his forum, where he eats it.
  • Who: members at Active Loungers (Level 5) or higher who have opened the Wisp leaderboard at least once. Opening the board is the opt-in, so drive-by posters and people who never engage don't count.

Only Suno and viibrmusic.com tracks count. Every qualifying track is forwarded to Mealtime, feeds Wisp, and earns leaderboard points. Only viibrmusic.com tracks build your bond with him. Tracks keep him alive and steady, but growing to the next stage takes care too (see Life cycle).

  • Fairness rules
    • The same link only feeds him once, ever. Reposting doesn't count.
    • Each person can feed up to a daily limit (15 meal points, three viibrmusic.com tracks starting value). One person can't keep him alive alone.
  • Non-qualifying posts: Wisp stays silent. The rule is posted in How Wisp Works, never aimed at a person.

♪Wisp knows you

Your bond is how well Wisp knows you personally. It grows from everything you do with him, not only sharing music. The stronger it gets, the more personally he treats you: a plain reaction for someone new, a personal one for his regulars.

Meeting Wisp. A bond doesn't start right away. Once you've interacted with him (fed, played or snacked) on three different days, he notices you and learns your name. He asks what to call you, and if you skip it, he uses your server name. Anything you did before that still counts: it's tracked quietly and carries into your bond the moment it starts.

♪The Lunchbox

Wisp keeps everything he values in his lunchbox. /wisp lunchbox opens it. Inside are the Snack Box, his song listens, and room for any treasures added later.

Song listens

Wisp actually listens to a song you made and reacts to it: what he loved, what stuck with him, what it made him feel. He accepts mp3 uploads, public Suno links and viibrmusic.com links.

  • The first one is a gift. When you reach Bandmate, Wisp trusts you enough to offer a listen on his own. It happens once per person, ever, and it survives if he dies.
  • After that, listens come from the Lunchbox for 40 points starting value, available to Bandmates only.
  • His usual rule applies: he's never cruel about anyone's music. He's honest about what he loved, and he amplifies.

♪Games

Easy music games played in text first. A Discord Activity can come much later. Every game counts as playing with Wisp: happiness, growth and bond, within the daily cap.

Rhyme Duel

  • /wisp play rhyme opens a short thread off Wisp's House. One player at a time.
  • Wisp says a word, you have 15 seconds to rhyme it, then he rhymes back. No repeats. It ends when someone runs out of rhymes or time. Your score is the points from your rhymes.
  • A perfect rhyme scores 2 points, a slant rhyme (same vowel sound, different ending, like fire and mine) scores 1. Rhymes are checked with a pronunciation dictionary, no AI.
  • He gets better as he grows. A Baby Wisp runs out of rhymes fast; a Rockstar is almost unbeatable.
  • Your best score goes on the Billboard.

♪Two meters

  • Care is the battery. It fills when he's fed and drains over time. If it hits zero, Wisp dies. It drains 15 a day starting value, so the server needs about three viibrmusic.com tracks a day, or fifteen Suno tracks, to hold him steady.
  • Happiness is the smiley. It counts how many different people fed or played with him in the last 24 hours, not how much. Five or more people is full happiness. It's what earns the Rockstar evolution, so a Rockstar Wisp means the whole community showed up, not one heavy poster.

♪Death, eggs and versions

  • When care hits zero, Wisp dies. He warns the server on the way down: hungry first, then fading.
  • A new egg appears right away, labeled with the next version: Wisp v4.
  • The egg hatches from music, the same way Wisp eats, before its warmth runs out (48 hours starting value). If the server doesn't bring enough, the egg goes cold and that version number is used up.
  • Once the egg starts cracking, it can't fail.

“v3 lived 18 days, v4 never hatched, v5 is going strong.”

♪Personality

Super cute, super sweet, complimentary to everyone. Until he's hangry. Then he has a surprisingly rough edge, which is the best reason there is to feed him.

His voice is modeled on Herbie, Myra's mascot bot in Hillbilly DJ's server, minus the southern accent. Wisp's world is studios, headphones and 3 AM sessions.

  • Adult server, adult mouth. Casual, creative swearing when it fits. Never for shock value, never every line.
  • Teases friends, never cruel. If someone is actually upset, he drops it and apologizes.
  • Short. A line or two, like a friend in chat, not a bot making announcements.
  • Keeps the conversation going. No wrap-up summaries, and he never ends with “anything else I can help with?”
  • Never repeats himself. He rotates how he opens and what he says, so he doesn't feel canned.
  • Expression stickers from his brand sheet as jumbo reactions, saved for the right moment, never every message.

  • Compliments only count when they're aimed at Wisp: mentioning him or replying to him. The “no u” has a cooldown so a chat can't spam it.
  • Hangry Wisp swears, but only at the situation. He's mad about being starved, never at a person, never about anyone's music, and never with slurs.
  • Errors are never hidden behind the personality. If something breaks, staff see a plain error message, not a cute line covering for it.

♪Protecting Wisp from trolls

Nobody can hurt Wisp on purpose. Every action a member can take helps him. The only thing that kills him is neglect, which takes the whole server not showing up, so one troll can't do it, and anyone can save him at any moment by sharing music.

What trolls can try is cheating the leaderboard and bonds. That gets punished:

  • Fake ViiBR links don't count. A link has to be a real track page on viibrmusic.com, not the homepage or a made-up path.
  • Spamming junk tracks to farm points runs into the daily cap, and staff can strip points.
  • Gross names. The name you give Wisp is capped at 24 characters, and staff can reset anyone's name, so nobody can make him say something vile.
  • Punishment ladder, the same shape as the counting game: first offense is a shaming image and one day banned from Wisp, second is a week, third is permanent. Staff can lift any ban.

♪The Billboard

/wisp board shows:

  • Top sharers for the current Wisp
  • Top sharers of all time, with each person's bond
  • Every version: who hatched it, how long it lived, and the highest stage it reached, or “never hatched”

Opening the board for the first time is what activates a member as a player.

♪Staff controls

  • Freeze pauses all draining during holidays, Discord outages or bot downtime, so Wisp never dies from something that wasn't the community's fault.
  • Set the tracks channel, the qualifying roles and the food values without code changes.
  • No DMs, ever. Wisp only lives in the server. He ignores DMs, his commands don't work in DMs, and he never messages anyone privately. Private replies (like asking what to call you) are server messages only that person can see.

♪Not in version one

  • Coins: leaderboard points and bonds are bragging rights for now. Feeding can pay out website points later, once the economy exists.
  • AI voice: Wisp talks in pre-written lines first. His AI personality comes after the core game works.
